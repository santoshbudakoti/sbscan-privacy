Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Summary

SB Scan works on your device. Your scans, PDFs, signatures, scanned codes and settings stay on your phone or tablet. We do not run servers for this app, we do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What the app stores

Scans and PDFs you create or open are saved in the app's private storage. Saved signatures, QR/barcode history and preferences are kept there too. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting a scan or PDF in the app removes its files. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner or QR screens are open, to capture pages and read codes. Images are processed on the device. We never receive them.

Photos, files and sharing

You choose which photos or PDFs to import using the Android picker; the app only receives the items you pick. Files are shared or saved only when you use Share, Save to Downloads or Save to Gallery.

On-device text recognition and barcode reading

Text recognition (OCR), barcode reading, edge detection and PDF processing run on the device. They use Google ML Kit on-device models, OpenCV and Apache PDFBox. Google ML Kit may send limited technical diagnostics (not your documents or images) to Google as described in the ML Kit terms.

App lock

If you turn on App lock, the app asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock using Android's system dialog. The app never sees or stores your biometric data or PIN.

Links you open

Opening a scanned web link, phone number, email, map or calendar entry hands it to the matching app on your device. Check codes from unknown sources before opening them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete any scan or PDF, clear the scan history, remove saved signatures from the signature dialog, or erase everything from Settings › Storage › Delete all data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the support address on the app's Google Play page.
